Founded by Syed Ansar Hyder Abedi in 1984, St. Meesum Mission High School was established to uplift educational standards and support the underprivileged. Inspired by Hazrat Meesum-e-Tammar, a symbol of truth and righteousness, we are committed to nurturing both academic excellence and strong moral values.

Our Mission

To provide accessible quality education and help every student reach their full potential through academic excellence and moral development.

Our Vision

To uplift educational standards and make quality learning accessible to students from all backgrounds in our community.
